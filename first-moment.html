✦   Our First Moment   ✦

💖 You know, sitting beside you was honestly the best part of my school days. We would talk about the most random things, laugh for no reason, and I would always find some excuse to playfully tickle you just to see that cute smile and hear your laughter. 😊✨

Back then, I was too young to understand what I was feeling. I didn't know if it was love or just a crush, but I knew that being around you made my heart feel different. Every moment spent with you felt special in a way I couldn't explain. ❤️

Looking back now, I realize those simple little moments — sharing a bench, talking, laughing, and just being together — became some of the most beautiful memories of my childhood. 🌸💕
